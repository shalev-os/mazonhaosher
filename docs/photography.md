Use Almog's actual work. Do not substitute generated, stock or recreated products.

## Original content used in the catalogue

- `hanukkah-2026-original.jpg`: the owner's supplied screenshot of Almog's 2026 doughnut collection flyer. The seasonal section preserves the original image and displays the flyer rectangle (x=0, y=247, width=590, height=786) through a CSS window, excluding phone and Facebook interface chrome. The four photographed flavours are coffee, Kinder, chocolate and pistachio; strawberry jam and dulce de leche are listed from the owner's accompanying text without invented product photographs.

- `source-image.jpg`: existing branded original with the Mazon HaOsher logo, Almog's name and phone, and labelled Kinder, red Bamba, cornflakes and pretzel cookies. Its old "weekly flavours" wording is not used as a current availability claim.
- New red-Bamba, cornflakes and pretzel views are CSS display crops of that same original, not new photographs or retouched recreations. `CataloguePhoto.tsx` preserves source pixels and uses the same source window in tiles and the enlarged viewer.
- `cookies-collection-2.jpg`: existing original tray photograph, consistent with `cookies-collection.jpg` and `cookies-collection-3.jpg`. Listed as an assortment, not invented individual flavours.
- Cake, dessert-box and main fruit photographs were collected from Almog's profile during the earlier authorised content work.
- Existing pistachio and Kinder photographs remain from the retained catalogue. Do not infer ownership of other files from similar filenames or product names.
